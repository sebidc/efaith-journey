# Bayan

This activity helped me realize that everyone has their own struggles, including me. Sometimes I struggle with self-doubt, distractions, laziness, and  even losing motivation, especially when things become overwhelming and I become less and less driven. Sharing these experiences with my group mates made me feel less alone and helped me understand that it is okay to have weaknesses, and gained back my faith in myself.

Through the life of St. Augustine, I learned that our mistakes do not define who we are and that change is always possible. I realized that overcoming my weaknesses takes time, patience, discipline, and faith in God. Most importantly, this experience taught me to trust God, keep trying even when things are difficult, and be more understanding and supportive of the people around me.
