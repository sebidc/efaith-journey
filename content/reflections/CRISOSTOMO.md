# Crisostomo

One of the struggles I have faced in my life is doubting my own abilities. Whenever I am given an opportunity, especially something unfamiliar or challenging, I often focus more on the possibility of failing than succeeding. I overthink what might happen and worry that I am not good enough or that I might disappoint myself and the people who believe in me. Because of these doubts, there have been opportunities I chose not to take, and I still sometimes wonder, “What if I had tried?” I realized that my fear of failure has sometimes stopped me from discovering what I am capable of. At times, this self-doubt also affects my faith because I forget to trust that God can guide me when I am uncertain about myself and my future.

As I learned more about St. Augustine’s Confessions, I began to reflect more deeply on my own struggles. I appreciated St. Augustine’s honesty in reflecting on his weaknesses, mistakes, and uncertainties while searching for God and a meaningful life. His journey helped me recognize that my self-doubt has sometimes been the biggest thing holding me back. I realized that having faith does not mean I will never experience fear or doubt, but that I can continue moving forward while trusting God to guide me. St. Augustine’s story helped me see my mistakes and weaknesses not as reasons to give up, but as opportunities to grow in faith and become a better person. Most importantly, I learned that I should not allow fear to make my decisions for me. Instead, I should trust God and the abilities He has given me.

With this realization, I want to become more courageous in taking opportunities, even when I am uncertain of the outcome. Instead of always asking, “What if I fail?” I want to ask, “What if I succeed?” I will learn from the opportunities I have missed and use them as motivation to take chances when they come again. I also want to strengthen my faith in God by praying more often and taking time to read and reflect on Scripture. These practices can help me seek God’s guidance and find strength when I feel uncertain.

At the same time, I want my growth to have a positive influence on others. Since I know how it feels to doubt myself, I want to be more compassionate toward others who experience similar struggles. I can encourage them, listen to them, and remind them that mistakes and failures do not make them less capable. Through my actions, I hope to grow in faith and inspire others to trust God, show compassion, and continue moving forward despite their fears.
