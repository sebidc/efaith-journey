# Hernandez

Growing up, I was always known as the top student in elementary, consistently bringing home the highest marks in class. While that felt great back then, it secretly created a heavy expectation that I carried everywhere. Getting perfect grades became my standard, and as I grew older, I became terrified of falling short. I genuinely believed that if I ever made a mistake, people would look at me differently, withdraw their approval, or care about me less.

Carrying the constant pressure to never mess up eventually became exhausting. Confronting my own setbacks forced me to realize that I needed to change how I saw things. I learned that failing is not something to run away from or feel ashamed of because it’s actually one of the most important teachers we can have. Mistakes do not mean I am not good enough. Instead, they give me the real life lessons I need to grow, become stronger, and move forward.

Leaning on my faith played a huge part in changing my heart. Reflecting on 2 Timothy 1:7, which reminds us that God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and a sound mind, truly helped me conquer my deepest anxiety. Guided by the Lord and this powerful truth, I no longer worry about falling down, because I know His grace will catch me and lift me up to higher ground.
