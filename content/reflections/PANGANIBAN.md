# Panganiban

One of the struggles I experience is getting easily distracted from my responsibilities. There are times when I spend too much time on my phone or other forms of entertainment instead of focusing on my studies and tasks. Because of this, I sometimes delay important responsibilities and make choices that are not always helpful. I also realize that being curious about activities like gambling can become another temptation, even if it starts as something that seems harmless.

Reflecting on the life of St. Augustine helped me realize that people can change even after making wrong choices. Augustine experienced struggles and made decisions that were not aligned with the life God wanted for him, but he eventually recognized his mistakes and turned his life toward God. His story taught me that my weaknesses do not have to define my future. I can learn from my mistakes and choose to become a better person.

Because of this realization, I want to become more disciplined with my time and avoid situations that encourage unhealthy habits, including gambling. I will focus more on my studies, responsibilities, and hobbies that help me grow. I also want to strengthen my prayer life and ask God for guidance whenever I feel tempted. I learned that making better choices every day requires patience, discipline, faith, and a sincere desire to improve.
