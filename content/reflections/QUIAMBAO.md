# Quiambao

One of the struggles I have experienced in my life is my lack of confidence. Growing up, I was always the kid who remained quiet in class, avoided interacting with classmates, and was too shy to initiate conversations. Because of this, I missed many opportunities that came my way, as I was always worried that I would fail or embarrass myself. Even now, I still struggle to overcome this issue.

While Augustine initially relied purely on his self-confidence, he later realized that human beings are fragile and prone to failure when relying entirely on their own strength. What I learned from St. Augustine's life is that we do not need to rely solely on ourselves, but should instead seek God's grace.

Reflecting on real-life situations, doing things alone often feels exhausting and nearly impossible. However, working on something alongside someone else feels far more motivating and manageable. Similarly, with God, we do not have to handle everything on our own; we simply need to ask Him for guidance, have faith, and, of course, always do our best.
