# Roque

One of the struggles I have faced in my life is controlling my anger. There are times when I become easily frustrated, especially when things do not go the way I expect them to. When I get angry, I sometimes say things without thinking about how they might affect other people. Even when I know that I should stay calm, my emotions can sometimes take over, and I end up regretting my words or actions afterward. I have realized that anger can affect not only my relationships with others but also the way I see and understand situations. Sometimes, I focus too much on what made me angry instead of trying to understand the reason behind it. This has made me realize that I need to learn how to control my emotions instead of allowing my emotions to control me.

As I learned more about St. Augustine’s Confessions, I began to reflect on how he also struggled with his desires, emotions, and weaknesses. I appreciated how honest he was about the mistakes he made and how he recognized the parts of himself that needed to change. His journey helped me understand that becoming a better person does not happen instantly. It requires recognizing our weaknesses and making an effort to change them. I realized that controlling my anger does not mean ignoring my feelings, but learning how to respond to them in a better way. St. Augustine’s relationship with God also reminded me that I do not have to face my weaknesses alone. I can ask God for patience, wisdom, and strength whenever I struggle to control myself.

With this realization, I want to become more patient and thoughtful in the way I deal with difficult situations. Instead of immediately reacting when I am angry, I want to learn to pause, think, and understand what I am feeling before I respond. I also want to become better at admitting when I am wrong and apologizing when my actions hurt someone. To strengthen my faith, I will pray for patience and ask God to help me become more understanding toward others. I know that changing my behavior will take time, but I believe that every small effort can help me become a better person.

At the same time, I want my growth to have a positive influence on the people around me. Since I know how difficult it can be to control strong emotions, I want to be more understanding when others are struggling with their own feelings. Instead of responding with anger, I want to learn to listen and communicate calmly. I hope that by becoming more patient and compassionate, I can build better relationships with the people around me. Through my experiences and lessons from St. Augustine, I want to continue growing not only as a person but also in my relationship with God. Most importantly, I want to learn that true strength is not about always winning an argument or expressing my anger, but having the patience and courage to choose peace and forgiveness.
