# Tapiceria

One of the struggles I experience is being tempted by gambling. There are times when I become curious or think about taking risks because I want quick rewards. Sometimes, I fail to think about the possible consequences and focus only on what I might gain. I realized that this habit can affect my time, decisions, responsibilities, and relationships with others. It also made me understand that giving in to temptation can make it harder to develop self-control.

Reflecting on the life of St. Augustine helped me realize that people can change even after making wrong choices. Augustine experienced struggles and made decisions that were not aligned with the life God wanted for him, but he eventually recognized his mistakes and turned his life toward God. His story taught me that my weaknesses and mistakes do not define who I have to become. I can acknowledge my mistakes, learn from them, and choose to make better decisions.

Because of this realization, I want to practice greater self-control and avoid situations that encourage me to gamble. I will focus more on my studies, responsibilities, hobbies, and meaningful activities that can help me grow. I also want to strengthen my prayer life and ask God for guidance when I feel tempted. I learned that changing a harmful habit takes patience, discipline, faith, and the willingness to make better choices every day.
