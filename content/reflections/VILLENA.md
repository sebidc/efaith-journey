# Villena

As the youngest child in our family, I learned that everything we do, inside or outside the home, affects who we become and the people around us, especially our parents and siblings. Since I was young, I witnessed how difficult the responsibilities of those older than me. I saw my parents work hard, my siblings work toward their studies and personal lives, and our family work every day to provide everyday needs. Because of this, I learned to value time and not waste it on things that don't help me to grow. At the same time, I felt pressured and afraid that someday, as my parents grew older and my siblings started their own lives, I might be left alone at home.‎

‎Because of this fear, I felt that I had to hurry. I thought I needed to move faster, study harder, and catch up with my siblings. However, rushing made it difficult for me to give my best, especially in academics. Eventually, I realized that I did not have to rush because everyone has their own time to try, learn, work hard, and achieve their dreams. Each of my siblings has their own journey, and I learned that I should not compare myself to their achievements.

‎From that realization I learned to use my time to learn, prepare, and understand myself better. As a high school student, I still have many experiences and opportunities ahead of me. Instead of seeing my siblings’ achievements as something I need to chase, now I see them as role models and guides for my own journey. I also learned to appreciate their efforts, tiredness, and sacrifices for my needs. 

‎This lesson also changed how I see family. I realized that family is more than the people we live with, they are our inspiration, motivation, and guide in life. Like Augustine’s experience, I learned the importance of looking back on my experiences and reflecting on how they have shaped the person I am becoming. Through reflection, I began to understand my fears, appreciate the people around me, and recognize that I do not need to rush my journey. Because of this, I have learned to value my family and every moment I have with them, knowing that we cannot be together forever. Although we may eventually follow different paths, the lessons, experiences, and love they have given me will always remain a part of who I am.
